Who We Are

The Rohingya Language Centre is an independent initiative dedicated to preserving, documenting, and promoting the Rohingya language through digital technology, education, linguistic research, and community collaboration.

🌍 Our Mission

Preserve the Rohingya language and make it accessible to future generations.

🎯 Our Vision

Build the world's largest digital platform for Rohingya language resources.

🤝 Our Community

Work together with volunteers, researchers, educators, and native speakers around the world.
