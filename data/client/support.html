How You Can Help

The Rohingya Language Centre is a community-driven project. We welcome volunteers, educators, linguists, developers, researchers, translators, and donors to help us preserve and promote the Rohingya language.

💖 Donate

Support the development of dictionaries, language technology, and educational resources.

🙋 Volunteer

Contribute translations, proofreading, recordings, or technical expertise.

🤝 Partner

Collaborate with universities, NGOs, researchers, and technology organizations.
