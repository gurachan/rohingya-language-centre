About the Rohingya Language Centre

The Rohingya Language Centre is an independent initiative dedicated to preserving, promoting and advancing the Rohingya language through research, education and digital innovation.

Our mission is to develop comprehensive dictionaries, language corpora, AI technologies, voice resources, educational materials and digital archives that ensure the Rohingya language continues to thrive for future generations.

Our Mission

📖 Dictionary

Build the world's most comprehensive Rohingya dictionary.

📚 Corpus

Create a large digital corpus for research and education.

🤖 Research & AI

Develop AI tools and datasets for the Rohingya language.

🎤 Voice

Preserve authentic Rohingya speech through a voice corpus.

Our Core Projects

📖 Rohingya Dictionary

Building the largest online Rohingya dictionary with multilingual support.

📚 Language Corpus

Collecting books, articles, stories and example sentences for linguistic research.

🤖 AI Research

Creating datasets for translation, speech recognition, OCR and language technology.

🎤 Voice Corpus

Recording authentic Rohingya speech from speakers around the world.
