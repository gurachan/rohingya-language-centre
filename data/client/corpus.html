What is a Language Corpus?

A language corpus is a large digital collection of authentic texts and spoken language. It helps researchers, educators, linguists, and AI systems understand how a language is used in real life.

📚 Written Texts

Books, newspapers, stories, articles, poetry, educational materials and historical documents.

🎤 Spoken Language

Conversations, interviews, speeches, oral history and everyday communication.

🤖 AI & Research

High-quality language data that supports dictionaries, translation systems, speech recognition and AI research.

Why the Rohingya Corpus Matters

A high-quality language corpus is the foundation of modern language technology. It supports dictionary development, language learning, translation, speech recognition, artificial intelligence, and the long-term preservation of Rohingya language and culture.

🎓 Education

Helps students and teachers access authentic Rohingya learning materials.

🔬 Research

Provides reliable linguistic data for researchers and universities.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Supplies high-quality datasets for translation systems, speech recognition, OCR, and future AI applications.

Corpus Development Goals

Our long-term vision is to build one of the world's largest digital Rohingya language corpora for education, research, and artificial intelligence.
