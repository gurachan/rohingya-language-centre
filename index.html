Building the Digital Future of the Rohingya Language

The Rohingya Language Centre is developing dictionaries, language corpora, AI technologies, voice resources, and digital archives to preserve and advance the Rohingya language for future generations.

About the Rohingya Language Centre

The Rohingya Language Centre is an independent initiative dedicated to preserving, documenting and advancing the Rohingya language through education, research and digital innovation.

📖 Dictionary

Build the world's most comprehensive Rohingya dictionary with English and Rohingya support.

🤖 AI Research

Develop translation, OCR, speech recognition, and language technologies.

🎤 Voice Corpus

Preserve authentic Rohingya pronunciation and oral history through voice recordings.

Our Core Projects

📖 Dictionary

English ↔ Rohingya multilingual dictionary with Hanifi, Latin and English support.

📚 Corpus

Millions of words and example sentences for education, linguistic research and AI.

🤖 AI Technology

Machine translation, OCR, speech recognition, language models and NLP resources.

🎤 Voice

Build the world's largest Rohingya voice corpus for future speech technologies.
